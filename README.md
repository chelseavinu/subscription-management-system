# Brightloop Software — Subscription Management & Recurring Billing Platform

> **Odoo Hackathon 2026**  
> A unified platform to automate plan configurations, quote-to-subscription lifecycles, recurring invoice generation, and revenue reporting.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Brightloop Software sells recurring software licenses, add-ons, and product variants. This platform replaces manual spreadsheet-based tracking with an automated, role-aware recurring billing system designed to eliminate missed renewals, prevent duplicate billing, and provide real-time revenue analytics.

---

## 👥 Roles & Access Control
- **Admin**: Full control over staff accounts, product catalog, variant adjustments, billing plans, tax rules, and discount configurations.
- **Internal Staff (Sales/Ops)**: Manage day-to-day operations, convert quotation templates into active subscriptions, generate invoices, and record payments.
- **Customer**: Dedicated self-service portal to view active/past subscriptions, track upcoming dues, and view invoices.
